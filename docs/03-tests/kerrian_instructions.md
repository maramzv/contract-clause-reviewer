Hi Mara,

Here are the 10 contracts for the answer-key check, with exact steps. The zip is attached and the sheet and definitions are linked below.

## Why we're doing this

We will score the tool against labels that come with a public dataset of contracts (CUAD). Before we trust those labels, we want an independent check on a small sample. You mark 10 contracts yourself, from the contracts alone. Afterwards I compare your marks with the dataset's labels and we look together at every place they differ. This is a spot check of the labels, not a test of you. A difference doesn't mean either of us is wrong: it tells us where the definition needs to be clearer.

## What you have

- `test10_for_mara.zip`: 10 contracts as plain text files, named contract_01 to contract_10. They were drawn from the dataset at random, and the names tell you nothing about the contracts. They have no labels.
- Clause Definitions: says what counts as each of the five clause types.
- Marking sheet: one row for each contract and clause type, 50 rows in all.

## What to do

1. Read the definitions first. If any wording is unclear, tell me before you start marking, not after. Please add your name and the date on the "Read by" line.
2. For each contract, read the whole text and look for the five clause types: Assignment, Change of Control, Termination for Convenience, Renewal and Governing Law. Use only the definitions, not what you think a lawyer would call the clause.
3. In the sheet, fill the five rows for that contract:
   - **Found?** Yes or No.
   - **Quoted passage:** if Yes, copy and paste the exact words of the passage. Don't retype or shorten them. If the clause is in more than one place, paste each passage in the same cell and separate them with `||`.
   - **Where:** the section or clause number, because the files are text and have no page numbers.
   - **Notes:** anything you were unsure about, for example "could be a venue clause, not governing law". Write a note whenever a call was close, and especially when you chose No.
4. Mark Renewal for the renewal or extension term only. Don't mark notice periods for non-renewal. The definitions explain why.
5. Mark every contract on its own, from the text. Please don't look up the contracts or search for their titles because that would defeat the check.
