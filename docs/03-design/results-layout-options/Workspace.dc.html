8.3 Upon expiration or termination, Endorser shall cease all use of the Company Marks, and Company shall cease distribution of materials bearing Endorser's name, image or likeness, except for inventory on hand for a period of ninety (90) days.

9. ASSIGNMENT

9.1 This Agreement and the rights and obligations hereunder may not be assigned or transferred by Endorser without the prior written consent of Company. Company may assign this Agreement to any successor to all or substantially all of its business or assets to which this Agreement relates.

9.2 Any transfer of this Agreement or of any right under it to an affiliate of a party shall be treated as an assignment for the purposes of this Section 9, and any purported assignment in violation of this Section shall be null and void.

10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

10.1 Endorser is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner or agent of Company. Nothing in this Agreement creates a joint venture or fiduciary relationship between the parties.

10.2 Endorser shall be solely responsible for all taxes arising from the compensation paid under this Agreement.
