14. ASSIGNMENT

14.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

14.2 Any purported assignment in violation of this Section shall be void. A transfer of all or substantially all of a party's assets to an affiliate shall be treated as an assignment for the purposes of this Section 14.

14.3 This Agreement shall be binding upon and shall inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

15. NOTICES

15.1 All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed given when delivered by hand, one (1) business day after being sent by a nationally recognized overnight courier, or three (3) business days after being sent by registered mail to the address set out on the signature page.

15.2 A party may change its address for notices by giving notice to the other party in accordance with this Section 15.
