Two guarantees

100%

Verbatim quotations

A quotation is shown only if it is found word for word in the contract. Anything else is dropped.

100%

Visible gaps

Every contract and category without a verified finding is labeled for review. A missing result is never read as absence.

Both are enforced by the verify step and the status labels, not by the model.

5

These are guarantees by construction. The verbatim check normalizes whitespace and requires at least 15 characters. They say nothing about whether the model found every clause: that is what the test results measure. They guarantee that what is shown is real contract wording, and that a gap is always visible to the reviewer.
